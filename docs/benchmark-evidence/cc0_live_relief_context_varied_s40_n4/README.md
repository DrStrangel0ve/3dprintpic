# Varied public relief-context matrix at 30 mm

This exact live-API matrix extends the 30 mm relief regression beyond the two
private reference photos. It uses three CC0 MakeHuman identities, one
deterministic procedural multi-lobe object, three lighting profiles, two
structured backgrounds, a small off-axis face, a strongly turned face, and a
low-luminance eyewear case. MakeHuman documents that exported models and its
bundled assets are
[CC0](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/makehuman/faq/can_i_sell_models_created_with_makehuman.html).

The matrix design follows Photo2Relief's emphasis on the complete photographed
scene rather than a face-only crop. Recent discontinuity-aware normal
integration work also motivates measuring smooth-surface appearance separately
from hard subject/background boundary behavior:

- [Photo2Relief](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.11364)
- [Discontinuity-preserving Normal Integration with Auxiliary Edges](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/papers/Kim_Discontinuity-preserving_Normal_Integration_with_Auxiliary_Edges_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
- [Discontinuity-aware Normal Integration for Generic Central Camera Models](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/papers/Milano_Discontinuity-aware_Normal_Integration_for_Generic_Central_Camera_Models_ICCV_2025_paper.html)

## Measured fixture correction

The first exact run at `b7d1088e1481ad5cce8fecddd2d0cb63caffe9ef`
passed three of four rows. Only `occlusion_deoccluded` failed, on the eyewear
row. At opacity `0.42`, the synthetic band measured `0.3485` dark coverage and
`0.4301` connected-component coverage against the production `0.40` and `0.45`
floors. Its width was `0.8544` against the `0.60` floor. Face shape, gradients,
lighting, background, boundary, topology, and STL-shell checks already passed.

Lowering the detector floors admitted a connected low-luminance cast-shadow
negative control. That approach was rejected. The final revision
`37e920a475155e732f533e63c98b94b3570a3e16` restores the original production
floors and makes the public synthetic eyewear unambiguous by setting its
opacity to `0.60`. The exact final band measures `0.5774` dark coverage,
`0.6921` component coverage, and `0.8520` width. A dedicated connected-shadow
negative test remains below both coverage floors despite spanning the required
width. This is a fixture-contract correction, not a relaxation of production
face detection.
